What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website when you visit it. They are used to remember information about your visit — such as whether you are signed in — so that the website can function correctly and provide a better experience.

Cookies can be essential (required for the site to function), functional (improving your experience), or tracking/advertising (monitoring behaviour across sites). EcoTrack uses only essential cookies.

Cookies We Use

EcoTrack uses a single essential cookie for authentication. This cookie is strictly necessary for the platform to function — without it, you cannot remain signed in.

Cookie NamePurposeTypeDuration
ecotrack_token Stores your secure session token to keep you authenticated between page loads Essential / Authentication 7 days (or until you sign out)

This cookie has the following security attributes:

  • HttpOnly: Cannot be accessed by JavaScript, protecting against XSS attacks
  • SameSite=Strict: Not transmitted on cross-site requests, protecting against CSRF attacks
  • Secure (production): Only transmitted over HTTPS encrypted connections in production environments
  • Scoped: Only sent to the EcoTrack domain — not to any third party
No consent required: Because this cookie is strictly necessary for the platform to function, it does not require your separate consent under applicable regulations. You cannot use EcoTrack while signed in without this cookie.

What We Do Not Use

EcoTrack does not use:

  • Analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics, Mixpanel)
  • Advertising or marketing cookies
  • Social media tracking pixels (e.g. Facebook Pixel)
  • Third-party behavioural tracking of any kind
  • Local storage or IndexedDB for user identification or tracking
  • Fingerprinting techniques

No data about your browsing behaviour, device characteristics, or usage patterns is shared with any advertising or analytics service.

Cookie Control

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View all cookies stored by websites you have visited
  • Delete individual or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Receive a notification when a cookie is set

Please note that if you delete or block the EcoTrack authentication cookie (ecotrack_token), you will be signed out and will need to sign in again to use the platform. No other functionality is affected by blocking other cookies, as EcoTrack does not use any.

Browser-specific cookie management instructions are available from your browser's help documentation:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Apple Safari

Third-Party Cookies

EcoTrack does not embed any third-party scripts, iframes, or resources that would set cookies from external domains.

The Inter font family is loaded from Google Fonts. Google Fonts may set cookies or log requests as part of their service. EcoTrack is a client of Google Fonts but has no control over Google's data practices. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy.

If you click any WhatsApp links within EcoTrack (e.g. for password reset or support), you will be redirected to WhatsApp's service, which operates under its own privacy and cookie policies.

Policy Changes

If EcoTrack introduces new cookies or tracking technologies in the future, this Cookies Policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised. Where optional tracking cookies are introduced, an appropriate consent mechanism will be provided before those cookies are set.

We will never introduce advertising or behavioural tracking cookies without clear notice and, where required by law, your explicit consent.
