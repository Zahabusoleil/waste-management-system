Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using EcoTrack, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree with any part of these terms, you must not use the platform.

These Terms apply to all users of EcoTrack, including residents, administrators, and collectors. Additional obligations specific to each role are described in the sections below.

The Service

EcoTrack is a waste management and collection tracking platform that enables residents to report waste issues, administrators to manage reports and collection assignments, and collectors to track and complete collection tasks.

The service is provided on an "as-is" basis. We make reasonable efforts to maintain availability and reliability but do not guarantee uninterrupted access. Scheduled or emergency maintenance may cause temporary service interruptions.

Important: EcoTrack is currently in development/testing phase. The platform has not been independently audited or penetration-tested. It should not be used as the sole system for critical public infrastructure management without appropriate professional evaluation.

User Accounts

You are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate, complete, and current information when registering
  • Maintaining the security of your login credentials
  • All activities that occur under your account
  • Notifying EcoTrack immediately of any unauthorised use of your account

Resident accounts are created through public registration. Administrator accounts are provisioned by the system operator. Collector accounts are created exclusively by administrators. Public registration does not grant administrator or collector privileges under any circumstances.

You must not create accounts using false identities, automated tools, or for the purpose of circumventing the platform's access controls.

Resident Responsibilities

As a resident using EcoTrack, you agree to:

  • Submit waste reports that are accurate, truthful, and relate to genuine waste issues
  • Provide correct location information to enable effective collection
  • Upload only photographs that you own or have the right to submit, and that depict actual waste
  • Not submit duplicate, false, misleading, or malicious reports
  • Not attempt to access other residents' reports or account information
  • Accept that approved or assigned reports cannot be edited or deleted until the collection process is resolved

Administrator Responsibilities

Administrators have elevated access to the platform and must:

  • Use administrator privileges only for legitimate platform management purposes
  • Keep administrator credentials strictly confidential and not share them with any other person
  • Create collector accounts only for verified, authorised individuals
  • Handle personal data of residents and collectors with care, in accordance with this platform's privacy policy and applicable Ugandan law
  • Not use administrator access to view, modify, or delete data for personal gain or in breach of user privacy
  • Approve or reject waste reports based on genuine assessment, not personal bias

Collector Responsibilities

Waste collectors using EcoTrack agree to:

  • Use the platform only for tasks assigned to their own account
  • Update task statuses accurately and in a timely manner
  • Provide truthful reasons when blocking a task
  • Not attempt to access, modify, or complete tasks assigned to other collectors
  • Not attempt to create, modify, or delete administrative records
  • Maintain the security of their account credentials

Prohibited Conduct

All users are prohibited from:

  • Attempting to gain unauthorised access to other accounts, the database, or the server
  • Submitting malicious code, SQL injection attempts, XSS payloads, or other attack vectors
  • Using automated scripts, bots, or scrapers against the platform without prior written consent
  • Uploading files that contain malware, executable code, or content other than genuine images
  • Circumventing rate limiting or other security controls
  • Using the platform in any way that violates Ugandan law or the rights of other users
  • Impersonating another person or organisation

Violation of these prohibitions may result in immediate account suspension and, where appropriate, referral to relevant authorities.

Because no payment service is provided, refunds are not applicable. If a commercial service tier is introduced in the future, a separate payment and refund policy will be published and users will be notified.
