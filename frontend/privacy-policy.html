Overview

EcoTrack is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we store and protect it, and what rights you have as a user of our platform.

This policy applies to all users of EcoTrack — residents, administrators, and collectors — and governs all personal data processed through the platform.

Key principle: We collect only the minimum personal data necessary to deliver the service. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data We Collect

When you use EcoTrack, we collect the following categories of personal data:

CategoryData CollectedWho Provides It
Account InformationFull name, email address, phone number, location/areaAll users at registration
Authentication DataHashed password (never plain-text), session token stored in a secure cookieAll users
Waste ReportsWaste category, location description, text description, optional photographResidents

We do not collect national ID numbers, financial information, biometric data, or sensitive personal data as defined under the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Creating and authenticating your account
  • Delivering the waste reporting and collection tracking service
  • Enabling administrators to assign and manage collection tasks
  • Sending password reset instructions via WhatsApp when requested
  • Maintaining audit logs for administrative accountability
  • Preventing abuse through rate limiting (IP-based, not stored permanently)
  • Improving the platform based on usage patterns (aggregate, not individual)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making that significantly affects you.

Data Storage & Security

All EcoTrack data is stored in a MongoDB database (MongoDB Atlas cloud service). The following security measures are in place:

  • Password hashing: All passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a cost factor of 10. Plain-text passwords are never stored.
  • Secure sessions: Authentication uses HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookies with a 7-day expiry. Tokens are not stored in localStorage.
  • Encrypted transit: All data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS encryption in production environments.
  • Access control: Database access is restricted to authorized application processes. No direct public database access is permitted.
  • Input validation: All submitted data is validated server-side before database storage.
  • Uploaded images: Photos are stored as binary data inside the database and served only to authorised users. They are not publicly accessible.
MongoDB Atlas: Data may be stored on servers outside Uganda as MongoDB Atlas is a cloud service. MongoDB Atlas maintains SOC 2 Type II certification and ISO 27001 compliance. For details, see the MongoDB Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data may be shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within the platform: Administrators can view resident names, emails, and report details as necessary to manage collections. Collectors see the name and report details of residents whose reports are assigned to them.
  • Service providers: MongoDB Atlas processes data on our behalf as a data processor. No other third-party data processors are currently used.
  • Legal obligations: We may disclose data if required by Ugandan law, court order, or to protect the safety of users or the public.

We do not integrate with any advertising networks, analytics services, or social media platforms that would receive your personal data.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account data: Retained for the life of your account. When an account is deleted by an administrator, associated reports and collection records are also permanently deleted.
  • Waste reports: Retained until the report is deleted by the resident (for Pending/Rejected reports), by an administrator, or upon account deletion.
  • Audit logs: Retained for up to 12 months for accountability purposes, then purged.
  • Password reset tokens: Automatically expire after 1 hour and are marked used after a single successful reset.
  • Session cookies: Expire after 7 days or upon logout.

Your Rights

Under the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, and the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate personal data. Residents can update some details through their dashboard; contact us for other corrections.
  • Right to erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data. Residents can delete eligible reports; account deletion requests should be submitted via WhatsApp.
  • Right to object: You may object to processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: You may request your data in a portable format where technically feasible.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp at +256 776 291 622. We will respond within 21 days as required by applicable Ugandan law.

Note: This policy is a project placeholder. It does not constitute certified legal compliance. Have a qualified Ugandan privacy/legal professional review it before deploying the application for real users.

Cookies

EcoTrack uses one essential cookie: the session authentication token. This cookie is:

  • HttpOnly — not accessible to JavaScript
  • SameSite=Strict — not sent on cross-site requests
  • Secure in production — only sent over HTTPS
  • Set to expire after 7 days

No analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking cookies are used. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform evolves. When material changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of EcoTrack after such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

For significant changes affecting your rights, we will provide notice through the platform where reasonably practicable.
