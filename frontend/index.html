About EcoTrack

Solving Waste Management Through Technology

EcoTrack creates a transparent workflow connecting residents, administrators, and waste collectors — from report submission to verified completion.

Residents

Register, submit waste reports with photos, track status and manage eligible reports from your personal dashboard.

Administrators

Review and approve reports, assign collectors, manage users and track all collections from a central control panel.

Collectors

Receive assigned tasks, update collection progress and record completion notes and observations.

Process

How It Works

Four simple steps from registration to resolved waste collection.

1

Create an Account

Residents register online in minutes. Collector accounts are provisioned by an administrator.

2

Report Waste

Submit a report with location, category, description and an optional photo from your dashboard.

3

Waste Collection

Approved reports are assigned to active collectors with a scheduled collection date.

4

Track Progress

Monitor task status and collection completion in real time from your personal dashboard.
