About EcoTrack

Solving Waste Management Through Technology

EcoTrack addresses the challenge of unmanaged waste in urban and peri-urban communities by creating a transparent workflow connecting residents, administrators, and waste collectors — from report submission to verified completion.

Residents

Register, submit waste reports with photos, track status and manage eligible reports.

Administrators

Review and approve reports, assign collectors, manage users and track all collections.

Collectors

Receive assigned tasks, update collection progress and record completion notes.

What We Offer

Our Services

Every step of waste management covered in one unified platform.

Waste Reporting

Residents submit waste issues with location, category, description and optional photo for fast action and tracking.

Collection Management

Administrators review reports, approve them and assign collection teams with scheduled dates from a central dashboard.

Collection Tracking

Collectors and residents track progress from assignment through in-progress to verified completion in real time.

How It Works

Four simple steps from registration to resolved waste collection.

Create an Account

Residents register online in minutes. Collector accounts are provisioned by an administrator.

Report Waste

Submit a report with location, category, description and an optional photo from your dashboard.

Waste Collection

Approved reports are assigned to active collectors with a scheduled collection date.

Track Progress

Monitor task status and collection completion in real time from your personal dashboard.
